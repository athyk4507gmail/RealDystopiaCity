## 🎨 Design Highlights

### Visual Identity
- **Black**: `#000000` - Primary background
- **Off-White**: `#f2f1ea` - Alternating sections
- **Ghost Text**: Reveals from 8% to 95% opacity
- **Typography**: Geist Sans (display), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (code)

### Signature Feature: Ghost Text Reveal
Large decorative labels (AI, ML, XGB, 12B, RT, SIM) that appear nearly invisible and reveal dramatically on hover. This creates a "wow" moment and adds depth to the design.

### Animation Philosophy
- **Fast (200ms)**: CTA buttons - immediate feedback
- **Medium (300ms)**: Card interactions - smooth
- **Slow (400ms)**: Ghost text - dramatic reveal
